RAFT VISUALIZER

The consensus algorithm inside etcd, Consul, and Kubernetes — leader election and log replication, animated. Kill the leader mid-write and watch the cluster recover without losing data. This is what "build your own distributed system" teaches.

What's happening: 5 nodes start as followers with randomized election timeouts. The first to time out becomes candidate, requests votes, and wins with a majority (3/5) — that's the whole safety trick: two leaders can't both get a majority in the same term. The leader then replicates every write to follower logs and only commits once a majority acks. Kill the leader and a new election fires automatically; uncommitted entries may be lost, committed ones never are. The simulation is ~200 lines below — open devtools → sources. Real Raft adds persistence and snapshots on top of exactly this.
