CAESAR-CRACK CHALLENGE

A secret message was encrypted with an unknown Caesar shift (1–25). Brute-force all 25 shifts and spot the English one — exactly how real cryptanalysis starts (just with much bigger key spaces).

Why this matters: password hashing is why a database leak doesn't reveal passwords — the salt defeats rainbow tables, and the one-way hash means the server never stores your secret. Classical ciphers show the two core ideas of all encryption: confusion (substitution) and the key space (why Caesar falls to brute force but AES-256 doesn't — 25 keys vs 2²⁵⁶). All crypto here runs on SubtleCrypto and ~60 lines of JS below — open devtools → sources.
