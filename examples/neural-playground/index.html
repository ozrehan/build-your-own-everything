What's happening: a 2→H→1 multilayer perceptron with tanh activations. Each training step: forward (weighted sums + tanh) → loss (binary cross-entropy) → backward (chain rule gives every weight its gradient) → update (gradient descent). No libraries — the whole network is ~70 lines of plain JS below. Try the spiral: a straight line can't solve it, but 6 hidden neurons can. That's why depth matters. Open devtools → sources.
